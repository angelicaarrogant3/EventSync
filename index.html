Help

Common questions from students and guests.

Do I need to install an app?

No. EventSync works in your web browser.

Where is my QR code?

Log in, open My Registrations, and tap View QR.

I lost my QR code. What now?

Log in again. Your QR is always saved under My Registrations.

What if my phone has no signal at the event?

Save your QR as a picture before you go, or ask the staff to look you up by your school ID.

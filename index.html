End-to-End Flow

How EventSync works

1

Register

Participants create an account and reserve a seat on any published event.

2

Get your QR

A unique QR pass is generated immediately and saved under My Registrations.

3

Scan at the door

Authorized admins scan passes at the entrance with instant valid/duplicate feedback.

4

Monitor live

Attendance rates and check-in feeds update across every admin screen in real time.

5

Export reports

Download formatted Excel attendance sheets or print-ready A4 PDFs.
